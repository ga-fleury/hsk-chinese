# HSK Chinese

A mobile app for learning the HSK 1 Chinese vocabulary (simplified characters). Built for personal study toward the HSK exam.

## Language

**Word**:
A single HSK 1 vocabulary entry: simplified hanzi, pinyin (with tone marks), and English gloss.
_Avoid_: Card, term, item

**HSK 1**:
Band 1 of the HSK 3.0 standard (2021) — 500 words. Not the classic HSK 2.0 150-word list.
_Avoid_: HSK level 1 (ambiguous between standards)

**Review**:
One flashcard exposure of a Word that the learner grades (Again / Hard / Good / Easy). Grades feed the SM-2 scheduler.
_Avoid_: Repetition, rep

**Due**:
A Word whose next scheduled review date is today or earlier. The review deck is all due Words plus a daily allotment of new ones.

**Quiz**:
A multiple-choice session (hanzi→meaning, meaning→hanzi, pinyin matching, or listening) scored right/wrong; quizzes do not affect SM-2 scheduling.
_Avoid_: Test, exam (reserved for the real HSK exam)

**Phrase**:
An everyday sentence built from HSK 1 Words, in the manner of HSK 1 textbook exercises. Carries a Chinese form, its pinyin, and an English translation.
_Avoid_: Sentence, expression

**Translation**:
A session in which the learner is shown a Phrase in English and types it in Chinese characters. Distinct from a Quiz: the answer is produced, not selected, and Translation keeps its own progress separate from both Quiz stats and Word scheduling.
_Avoid_: Translation quiz, writing test

**Attempt**:
One graded answer to a Phrase during a Translation. Auto-graded against the Phrase's accepted answers, and overridable by the learner when their answer was valid but unanticipated.
_Avoid_: Try, submission

**Gloss**:
An inline translation of a word in a Phrase that falls outside HSK 1. Glossed words are given to the learner with the Phrase, never assumed known and never tested.
_Avoid_: Hint, note, vocabulary aid

**Theme**:
The everyday situation a Phrase belongs to (greetings, family, time, food, shopping, directions). Every Phrase has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Category, topic, lesson
